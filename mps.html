Managed Print Services (MPS) & Device Leasing

All-in-one printing, toner, and support — with predictable costs.

Print Without Stress, Downtime, or Surprise Expenses

Managing printers across a growing office can be frustrating — toner runs out, machines break, and costs become unpredictable. Coast Infrastructure Systems Limited provides Managed Print Services (MPS) so your team can print smoothly while you control spend.


We deploy and manage the right devices for your needs, provide consumables, handle repairs, and track usage — all under a simple service plan. We also offer device leasing for printers and essential office IT equipment.

Our MPS & Leasing Services Include

Printer Supply & Deployment

We recommend and deploy the right printers/MFPs based on your volume, workflow, and budget.

Cost-Per-Page Billing

Predictable monthly billing based on usage — ideal for offices that want controlled printing costs.

Toner & Consumables Management

Automated/managed replenishment so your team never gets stuck in the middle of critical work.

Maintenance & Repairs (Included)

Preventive maintenance and quick fixes to reduce downtime and extend device lifespan.

Network Printing & User Setup

Driver setup, printer sharing, scan configuration, and smooth access for teams.

Device Leasing

Lease printers and essential office devices for flexible operations — great for startups and expanding teams.
