Solar Energy Solutions

Reliable Power. Lower Bills. Peace of Mind.

Power Your Home or Business with Confidence

Unstable electricity slows down business, damages equipment, and adds stress to daily life. At Coast Infrastructure Systems Limited, we design and install clean, efficient solar and inverter systems that keep your critical appliances running — day and night.


From site survey and load assessment to installation, protection, and after-sales support, we deliver a system that matches your needs, your budget, and your future growth.

Our Solar Services Include

Solar System Design & Installation

Custom system sizing, panel mounting, wiring, and clean installation for homes, offices, and facilities.

Inverter & Battery Setup

Hybrid/off-grid inverter installation with battery configuration for stable backup power.

Load Assessment & Power Prioritisation

We identify your critical loads and design a system that powers what matters most — efficiently.

Protection & Safety Installations

Proper breakers, surge protection, earthing, and safe cabling to protect your investment.

Monitoring & Optimization

Performance checks and configuration to improve battery life, reduce losses, and boost uptime.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Panel cleaning advice, system inspection, fault diagnosis, repairs and upgrades when needed.
