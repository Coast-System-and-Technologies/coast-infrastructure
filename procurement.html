At Coast Infrastructure Systems Limited, we understand that great infrastructure depends on quality components. That s why we offer reliable procurement and sourcing services for a wide range of technology and infrastructure related products

What We Procure

We handle the procurement of a wide range of certified, high performance equipment, including:

Power & Energy

  • Solar panels (monocrystalline, polycrystalline)
  • Inverters (1kVA-20kVA, hybrid, smart)
  • Deep cycle and lithium batteries
  • Charge controllers, breakers, and accessories

Security & Surveillance

  • CC TV cameras (IP, dome, bullet, PTZ)
  • DVRs/NVRs and hard drives
  • Surveillance accessories (cables, connectors, adapters)

Networking & Connectivity

  • Routers , switches, and access points
  • Ethernet and fiber optic cables
  • Patch panels, faceplates, and racks

General Tech Infrastructure

  • Smart devices and IoT hardware
  • Installation tools and accessories
  • Office tech (printers, UPS, modems)

Digital Assets

  • Domain names (standard and premium)
  • SSL certificates
  • Software licensing (by request)
