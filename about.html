Who We Are

Coast Insfrastucture Systems Limited (CISL) is a technology-driven infrastructure company devlivering practical, reliabl, and forward-looking solutions for power, security, connectivity, and digital access.

we help individuals, SMEs, and enterprises stay powered, protected, and prepared whether through solar installations, smart surveilance, domain provisioning, networking, or Procurement of essential tech components.

As a proud subsidiary of Coast System and Technologies Limited, we combine deep industry knowledge with execution excellence to bring infrastructuresolutions to life across Nigeria and beyond.
